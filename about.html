About this site

Tech Help Made Simple publishes practical checklists for UK households and small offices. The goal is to help readers define the problem, check compatibility and avoid unnecessary purchases.

We do not claim to have hands-on tested any product unless an individual guide explicitly says so. No manufacturer has paid for placement here. Some guides have clearly labelled affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission; that does not change the price you pay to us.

Guides explain general buying principles, not a guarantee that a particular product will work with your devices. Check the seller’s current specifications and terms.
