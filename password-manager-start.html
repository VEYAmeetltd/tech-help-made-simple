Start using a password manager safely

A password manager can help you use unique passwords. The transition is easier when you begin with your most important accounts and plan for losing access to your main device.

Check before you spend

  1. Choose a reputable service with strong account recovery options and support for your devices. Read its export and emergency-access options.
  2. Create a long, unique master password and enable multi-factor authentication. Keep recovery codes somewhere separate and private.
  3. Replace reused passwords gradually, beginning with email, banking and your primary shopping accounts.

The trade-off

A built-in manager may be simplest inside one ecosystem; a separate app can be more flexible across platforms. The important test is whether you will actually use it consistently.

First useful step

Set up the manager on two devices, save recovery information, and practise signing in before migrating everything. Never email your master password to yourself.
