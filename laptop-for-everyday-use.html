PRACTICAL TECH GUIDE / 04

Pick an everyday laptop by the work you do

The right laptop is the one that handles your normal tasks comfortably for several years. Start with your software, portability needs and repair expectations rather than a discount badge.

Check before you spend

  1. Write down the programmes and browser tabs you actually use. Check operating-system compatibility for any specialist software.
  2. Try the keyboard, screen and trackpad in person when possible. You interact with these every day, even when processor benchmarks look good.
  3. Check memory, storage, port selection, warranty and whether battery replacement or repair is practical.

The trade-off

A very light machine may trade ports and cooling for portability. A large screen may be easier to work on but unpleasant to carry. Neither is better without your use case.

First useful step

Make a must-have list and a maximum price. Compare two or three models against that list, then verify the exact specification on the manufacturer page before ordering.
