PRACTICAL TECH GUIDE / 11

Plan travel charging around the devices you own

One charger can sometimes replace several plugs, but labels can hide how power is split between ports. Build the kit around your actual phone, laptop and travel pattern.

Check before you spend

  1. Check the charging requirements of each device and whether they use USB-C Power Delivery or a proprietary connector.
  2. Read the charger output for simultaneous use, not only the largest single-port number.
  3. Use sound, undamaged cables rated for the power you need, and check plug compatibility at the destination.

The trade-off

A small multi-port charger saves bag space but can reduce laptop power when a phone is plugged in. A spare cable may be more valuable than a higher headline wattage.

First useful step

Lay out every device before a trip and test the whole kit at home. Keep a simple list so you can replace one missing cable without buying another charger.
