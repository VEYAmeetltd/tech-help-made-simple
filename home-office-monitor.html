Set up a comfortable home-office monitor

A monitor upgrade should make text easier to read and the desk easier to use. Size alone does not guarantee comfort; viewing distance, resolution, positioning and connections all matter.

Check before you spend

  1. Measure your available desk depth and where your eyes naturally land. Leave enough room to position the screen without crowding the keyboard.
  2. Check native resolution and scaling on your computer. A sharp panel can still look poor if the connection or settings force a lower mode.
  3. Check stand adjustment or mounting options, input ports and whether your laptop supports the desired display mode.

The trade-off

An ultrawide can replace two screens for some workflows but makes screen sharing and window layout different. Two smaller screens offer flexibility but need more cables and desk space.

First useful step

Mock up the screen width with paper on your desk. Test your normal text size and application layout in a shop if possible before committing.
