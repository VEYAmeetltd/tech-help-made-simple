PRACTICAL TECH GUIDE / 03

Choose an external SSD without paying for speed you cannot use

Portable SSD boxes advertise very high transfer rates. Your computer port, cable and workload decide whether you will see them. Capacity, reliability and backup habits often matter more.

Check before you spend

  1. Check the exact USB or Thunderbolt capability of the port you will use; a familiar connector shape does not guarantee a particular speed.
  2. Estimate your current files and allow room for growth. A drive used for backups needs enough space for more than one generation of files.
  3. Read the warranty and confirm the included cable works with your devices. Keep a second copy of anything you cannot replace.

The trade-off

Small file transfers, photo libraries and occasional backups seldom need the highest advertised peak speed. Paying for a premium interface makes sense only when the whole chain supports it.

First useful step

List your devices and the largest project you move regularly. Choose capacity first, then an interface your actual ports support, then compare reputable sellers and return terms.
