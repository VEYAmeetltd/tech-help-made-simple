PRACTICAL TECH GUIDE / 09

Free phone storage without losing important files

A full phone can be stressful, especially when it contains photos and messages. Start by understanding what uses the space and confirm your backup before clearing anything irreplaceable.

Check before you spend

  1. Open the storage breakdown and identify the largest categories. Downloads, offline videos and app caches may be easier wins than photos.
  2. Verify that recent photos exist in a second location and that you can open them there before deleting local copies.
  3. Review large apps and saved media. Clear only content you can re-download or have separately backed up.

The trade-off

Buying cloud space can help with backup and access, but it may not immediately free local storage. Some services keep local copies until you enable an optimisation setting.

First useful step

Take a screenshot of current storage, clear one safe category, then check the result. Avoid mass-cleaner apps that ask for broad access to private files.
