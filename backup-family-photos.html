A backup plan for family photos

Photos deserve a recovery plan you have actually tried. A sync service makes files available on more devices, but a mistaken deletion can also synchronise. Keep a separate copy you can restore.

Check before you spend

  1. Locate originals on phones, computers and old drives. Decide which library is the master before deleting duplicates.
  2. Keep one copy away from the main device: a cloud backup or an external drive stored separately. For important memories, use both.
  3. Check what the service backs up: full-resolution photos, videos, edits and shared albums can have different rules.

The trade-off

An external drive has a one-time cost but can fail or be lost. Cloud storage adds a recurring bill and needs an account you can recover. Neither removes the need to test a restore.

First useful step

Choose ten sample photos, back them up, then restore them to a different folder. Put a monthly reminder on your calendar and check that new photos still appear.
