Make home Wi-Fi more reliable

A faster broadband package will not fix every weak room. Work out whether the problem is the internet connection, the router, or the path between your device and the router before buying anything.

A backup plan for family photos

Photos deserve a recovery plan you have actually tried. A sync service makes files available on more devices, but a mistaken deletion can also synchronise. Keep a separate copy you can restore.

Choose an external SSD without paying for speed you cannot use

The right laptop is the one that handles your normal tasks comfortably for several years. Start with your software, portability needs and repair expectations rather than a discount badge.

The USB-C hub compatibility checklist

USB-C describes a connector, not a promise that every port supports video, charging or the same data speed. A hub only works as intended when the computer and hub support the same features.

Set up a comfortable home-office monitor

A monitor upgrade should make text easier to read and the desk easier to use. Size alone does not guarantee comfort; viewing distance, resolution, positioning and connections all matter.
